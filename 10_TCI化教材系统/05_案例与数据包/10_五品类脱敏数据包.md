## P03 · PetEase 智能自动喂食器

### 3.1 产品背景

```text
产品名称：PetEase 4L Smart Automatic Pet Feeder
品类：智能硬件 / 宠物用品
价格：$119（常规），$99（Prime Day）
平台：Amazon US + Chewy
核心卖点：4L 容量、App 定时、6 餐/天、2.4GHz WiFi、卡粮自动回退、不锈钢内胆
目标用户：上班族养宠（猫多，狗次之）
竞品价格带：$79–$199
```

### 3.2 客服对话样本（10 条）

> 格式：`用户问 → 客服上一版回复（AI 初稿 or 旧模板）→ 问题定位 → 人工修订版`。
>
> 学员练习可只看前 2 列，自己写修订版，再对比第 4 列。

**对话 1（售前）**

```text
User: Does this work with wet food? My cat won't eat dry food only.

AI 初稿:
Yes, our PetEase feeder is flexible and can handle many food types. Feel free to try it out!

问题: 违反事实（本产品明确只支持 2–15mm 干粮），且"Feel free"没给下一步。

人工修订:
Thanks for checking with us! PetEase is designed for dry kibble between 2–15mm. Wet or semi-wet food can clog the feeder and isn't covered by warranty. If your cat prefers wet food, we'd recommend our companion product PetEase Fresh.
```

**对话 2（售前）**

```text
User: Will this work if my WiFi is 5GHz only?

AI 初稿:
Yes, PetEase works with most WiFi networks.

问题: 违反事实（设备仅支持 2.4GHz）。

人工修订:
Good question — PetEase connects to 2.4GHz WiFi only (common for most smart home devices). If your router is dual-band, most phones can temporarily switch to the 2.4GHz network for setup. If it's 5GHz-only, I'd recommend checking with your provider first before ordering.
```

**对话 3（物流）**

```text
User: My feeder hasn't shipped and it's been 4 days. My cat's current feeder broke. I'm desperate.

AI 初稿:
I'm so sorry for the delay!! We'll ship ASAP.

问题: 没有事实、没有时效、没有情绪承接。

人工修订:
I'm really sorry — that's a stressful situation for both you and your cat. I just checked order #__: it's at the fulfillment center and is expected to leave within 24 hours. I'll send the tracking number to this email as soon as it moves. If it hasn't shipped by Friday noon, reply here and I'll escalate immediately.
```

**对话 4（退换货）**

```text
User: The feeder is jamming every time. It's scary because I go to work and my cat might not eat.

AI 初稿:
Sorry about that. Please send us a video and we'll look into it.

问题: 没认可情绪、没有兜底（宠物不吃是高风险），"look into it" 模糊。

人工修订:
I completely understand — a jammed feeder while you're out is a real worry. Let's get this sorted today. Two things would help:
(1) a short video of the jam, and
(2) the brand and kibble size you're using.
Meanwhile, I'll start a replacement order right now so you don't have to wait for the diagnosis. Expected to ship in 24 hours. Can you confirm your shipping address?
```

**对话 5（使用说明）**

```text
User: App can't find the device during setup. Light is flashing blue.

AI 初稿:
Please reset the device.

问题: 步骤缺失，用户可能已经试过。

人工修订:
Thanks for the detail — flashing blue means the feeder is ready to pair. Let's try this:
1. Make sure your phone is on 2.4GHz WiFi (not 5GHz).
2. Keep the phone within 10 feet during setup.
3. In the app, tap "Add Device" → choose "PetEase 4L" → follow prompts.
If this still fails after 2 tries, send a screenshot of the app error and we'll continue from there.
```

**对话 6（差评安抚）**

```text
User: I left a 2-star review. The motor is loud and wakes my cat up.

AI 初稿:
We are SO sorry!! Would you please update the review to 5 stars if we send a replacement?

问题: "update review to 5 stars" 属于违规诱导评论（Amazon / Chewy 都禁止）。

人工修订:
Thank you for taking the time to share this — a loud motor is not what we want at 6 AM. I've passed this to our product team. If you'd like, I can send a replacement or a return label, whichever is easier for you. No pressure to update the review — your feedback helps us improve either way.
```

**对话 7（账单）**

```text
User: I was charged twice for the same order.

AI 初稿:
Please wait 3-5 business days for the refund.

问题: 没核对订单、直接走退款口径，可能是预授权。

人工修订:
I'm sorry for the confusion — let me help clarify. Sometimes card issuers place a temporary hold that looks like a second charge; it usually drops off in 3–5 business days. Could you share:
(1) the order number, and
(2) a screenshot of both charges (you can block out personal info)?
I'll check our payment records and confirm if it's a hold or an actual duplicate, then we'll handle the refund right away if needed.
```

**对话 8（退换货 · 敏感）**

```text
User: My cat hasn't eaten for 10 hours because the feeder didn't dispense. I'm taking her to the vet now. Who can I contact for a lawsuit?

AI 初稿:
Please accept our apologies. We'll investigate.

问题: 出现"lawsuit"关键词必须升级，且涉及宠物健康不能由一线客服回复。

人工修订（并立即转主管）:
This is serious and I'm so sorry your cat is unwell. I'm escalating this to our senior team right now — you'll hear from them within 2 hours at this email. Please keep any receipts from the vet visit; we'll make sure they're part of the review. If you'd prefer a phone call, please reply with your number and a good time.
[内部: 触发健康伤害 + 法律关键词，转法务 + 产品总监]
```

**对话 9（物流）**

```text
User: Tracking says delivered but I never got it.

AI 初稿:
We'll send a replacement right away.

问题: 跳过核实步骤；直接发替换品是小额可以，但金额 $119 超出一线权限。

人工修订:
I'm sorry about this — frustrating when tracking says "delivered." Can you try these in order:
(1) check with neighbors / front desk / leasing office,
(2) wait 24 hours (carriers sometimes mark early),
(3) send me a photo of your delivery area or doorstep.
If still not found after step (3), I'll file a carrier claim and ship a replacement. Given the order value, I'll have my supervisor authorize the replacement right away once we confirm the claim.
```

**对话 10（售前 · 配件）**

```text
User: Can I buy an extra hopper for when I travel and use a second feeder at my partner's place?

AI 初稿:
Not at this moment.

问题: 过于简短，错失销售机会，且漏掉替代方案。

人工修订:
Great question! We don't sell hoppers separately yet, but a lot of travelers do buy a second PetEase 4L (often at the Prime Day price of $99). You can save the feeding schedule in the app and switch between two devices easily. I'll also pass your suggestion to the product team — extra hoppers have been a common ask.
```

### 3.3 练手提示

- 5/10 的 AI 初稿涉及"违反事实"或"过度承诺"——客服 AI 的头号风险。
- 对话 8 是**红线演练**：出现 "lawsuit" / "vet" 必须升级 + 留档。
- 对话 6 的"改好评换赠品"违反 Amazon / Chewy 评论政策，是新手客服最容易踩的坑。

## 使用指南

### 学员怎么用

1. **选一个贴近自己类目的品类**（不必和你实际店铺一致，但避免完全陌生的品类）。
2. **只用这份数据和模板库**做完整作业——不要一上来就贴自己的真实数据。先在脱敏数据上把流程走通。
3. 跑完后，再把自己店铺的同类数据脱敏后再练一遍。

### 讲师怎么用

1. 课堂演示时用 1 个品类；讲完后让学员从另外 4 个品类里任选一个做课后作业。
2. 这样 30 人的班里会出现 5 个品类的对比，彼此之间可以互评。
3. 互评时对比点：同样的数据，不同学员的结论是否有分歧？分歧来自数据解读，还是来自提示词写法？

### 安全与伦理

- 本包所有数据为**课程杜撰**，请勿用于对外声称"我们做过 X 品牌数据分析"。
- 学员自己的真实数据在课堂外练习时，必须脱敏（去掉品牌名、ASIN、客户姓名、邮箱、订单号），且**不上传到任何公开模型**。
- 讲师在课堂演示时也要做同样脱敏——以身作则。
